Unit Assessment: Ratios & Proportional Relationships

Grade 6 Math | Complete Assessment with Proficiency Rubric

⏱️ Duration: 90 minutes
📊 Total Points: 100
🎯 Standards: 6.RP.1-3

✏️ STATUS: DRAFT – Ready for SME review

Assessment Overview

This assessment measures understanding of all three standards in the unit:

  • 6.RP.1: Understanding ratios
  • 6.RP.2: Understanding unit rates
  • 6.RP.3: Solving ratio problems

Format

Part Type Time Points
A Multiple Choice (6 items) 15 min 20 pts
B Short Answer (3 items) 20 min 30 pts
C Performance Task (3 tasks) 40-50 min 50 pts

PART A: Multiple Choice (20 points)

Directions: Choose the best answer. Circle the letter.

Question 1 (6.RP.1 - BASIC)

In a classroom, there are 15 boys and 10 girls. What is the ratio of boys to girls?

  • A) 10:15
  • B) 15:10 ✓
  • C) 10 to 25
  • D) 15:25

Question 2 (6.RP.1 - INTERMEDIATE)

Which three expressions all represent the same ratio?

  • A) 3:1, 3 to 1, 3/1 ✓
  • B) 3:1, 1:3, 3/1
  • C) 3:1, 3 to 3, 3/3
  • D) 3:1, 1 to 3, 1/3

Question 3 (6.RP.2 - INTERMEDIATE)

A runner ran 20 miles in 4 hours. What is the unit rate (miles per hour)?

  • A) 4 miles per hour
  • B) 5 miles per hour ✓
  • C) 20 miles per hour
  • D) 24 miles per hour

Question 4 (6.RP.2 - ADVANCED)

Two stores have different prices:
Store A: 8 notebooks for $16
Store B: 6 notebooks for $12
Which store has the better price per notebook?

  • A) Store A ($2 per notebook)
  • B) Store B ($2 per notebook)
  • C) Both stores have the same price ✓
  • D) Cannot determine

Question 5 (6.RP.3 - INTERMEDIATE)

A recipe for 4 people uses 3 cups of rice. How much rice is needed for 12 people?

  • A) 6 cups
  • B) 7 cups
  • C) 9 cups ✓
  • D) 12 cups

Question 6 (6.RP.1 - ADVANCED)

A recipe uses 2 cups flour to 1 cup sugar. If you increase the recipe proportionally, what would be true?

  • A) The ratio would become 2:2
  • B) The ratio would stay 2:1 ✓
  • C) The ratio would change to 1:2
  • D) You would only need 1 ingredient

PART B: Short Answer (30 points)

Question 1 (10 points) - 6.RP.2

Three coffee shops have different drink sizes:
Shop A: 12 oz for $3
Shop B: 16 oz for $4
Shop C: 20 oz for $5

For each shop, find the unit rate (price per ounce). Then determine which shop offers the best value.

Expected Response:
Shop A: $0.25 per oz
Shop B: $0.25 per oz
Shop C: $0.25 per oz
All three have the same unit price.

Question 2 (10 points) - 6.RP.3

A paint mixture uses 2 parts red paint to 3 parts blue paint. If you have 10 gallons of red paint, how much blue paint do you need?

Show your work using a table, diagram, or equation.

Expected Response:
Red : Blue = 2 : 3
If red = 10 gallons, multiply by 5:
Blue = 3 × 5 = 15 gallons

Question 3 (10 points) - 6.RP.1

In a bag of marbles:
12 are red
8 are blue
5 are green

Write three different ratios. Use three different forms (colon, words, fraction) and identify whether each is part-to-part or part-to-whole.

Expected Response Examples:
1. Red to Blue: 12:8 (part-to-part)
2. Green to total: 5:25 (part-to-whole)
3. Blue to total: 8:25 (part-to-whole)

PART C: Performance Task (50 points)

Task A (15 points): Calculate Quantities Using Ratios

A recipe for lemonade serves 6 people and uses:
- 4 lemons
- 2 cups sugar
- 6 cups water

Your class has 24 people. Calculate how much of each ingredient you'll need for the party.

Show your work using at least TWO different strategies (table, tape diagram, number line, or equation).

Expected Response:
Since 24 ÷ 6 = 4, multiply each ingredient by 4:
- Lemons: 4 × 4 = 16 lemons
- Sugar: 2 × 4 = 8 cups
- Water: 6 × 4 = 24 cups

Task B (15 points): Compare Unit Rates and Make Decisions

Your teacher asks about buying cookies for the party. Three bakeries have different prices:
- Bakery A: 18 cookies for $9
- Bakery B: 24 cookies for $12
- Bakery C: 15 cookies for $6

1) Calculate the unit rate (price per cookie) for each bakery.
2) Which bakery offers the best value? Justify your answer.
3) If you need 72 cookies for the party, how much will it cost at the cheapest bakery?

Expected Response:
Bakery A: $0.50 per cookie
Bakery B: $0.50 per cookie
Bakery C: $0.40 per cookie
Bakery C is cheapest.
For 72 cookies: 72 × $0.40 = $28.80

Task C (20 points): Create Your Own Ratio Problem

Design your own ratio problem for the class party. Your problem should:
- Include a real-world context (decorations, supplies, costs, quantities)
- Require at least one ratio calculation
- Use at least one unit rate
- Include a decision or comparison

Write:
1) Your problem statement
2) Necessary information
3) Solution showing your work
4) Answer with explanation

Proficiency Rubric

Proficiency Level Score Range Description
ADVANCED 86-100 (A) Can analyze ratios in complex contexts, calculate and compare unit rates, solve multi-step problems using multiple strategies, and create accurate problems
INTERMEDIATE 71-85 (B) Can write ratios in multiple forms, calculate unit rates correctly, solve multi-step problems with clear strategy, and make decisions based on unit rates
BASIC 60-70 (C) Can identify and write ratios, calculate simple unit rates with support, and solve one-step ratio problems using one strategy
BELOW BASIC Below 60 (F) Struggles with ratio concept, unit rates, and problem-solving; requires intensive review and re-teaching

Using Assessment Results

For Grading

  • Part A: 20 points
  • Part B: 30 points
  • Part C: 50 points
  • Total: 100 points

For Instruction

  • Students below 60%: Plan small group re-teaching
  • Students 60-70%: Solidify basic understanding
  • Students 71-85%: Extend to more complex problems
  • Students 86-100%: Challenge with advanced contexts
