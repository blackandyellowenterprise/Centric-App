How-To Guides

Step-by-Step Workflows for All Team Members

For Educators: Using the Curriculum

How to Teach Lesson 1: Understanding Ratios

  1. Open Lesson 1 page
  2. Review learning objectives for your proficiency level
  3. Gather materials (tiles, chart paper, pizza image)
  4. Follow timeline: Hook (10 min) → Instruction (15 min) → Guided Practice (20 min) → Independent (12 min) → Exit Ticket (3 min)
  5. Use differentiation strategies for your students
  6. Administer exit ticket to assess understanding

How to Use the Assessment

  1. Open the Unit Assessment
  2. Choose format: Administer all parts or spread over 2-3 days
  3. Part A (Multiple Choice): 15 min - students select answers
  4. Part B (Short Answer): 20 min - students show work and explain
  5. Part C (Performance Task): 40-50 min - students apply learning to real scenarios
  6. Use proficiency rubric to score and determine student level
  7. Use results to plan small group instruction

For SMEs: Reviewing Curriculum

How to Review a Draft Lesson (20-30 min)

  1. Open lesson page and download or print
  2. Get the SME Review Checklist (see GitHub)
  3. Read through lesson quickly (5 min)
  4. Go section by section:
    • Standards Alignment: Does it match standard language?
    • Content Accuracy: Are examples correct?
    • Instructional Quality: Are activities engaging and doable?
    • Assessments: Do they measure objectives?
    • Practical Implementation: Will this work in your classroom?
  5. Mark approval status:
    • ☐ APPROVED – Use immediately
    • ☐ APPROVED WITH REVISIONS – Make these changes first
    • ☐ REQUEST REVISION – Major changes needed
  6. Submit completed checklist to project lead

Common Things to Check

  • ✓ Accuracy of all examples and calculations
  • ✓ Are activities appropriate for Grade 6?
  • ✓ Do timing estimates match your class?
  • ✓ Are real-world examples culturally appropriate for your students?
  • ✓ Do misconceptions addressed match what you see in your classes?
  • ✓ Are materials available/affordable?

For Project Managers: Coordinating Workflows

How to Run a Curriculum Generation Cycle (1-2 weeks)

  1. Week 1, Day 1-2:
    • Identify standards to use (e.g., Kansas Math Grade 6)
    • Request AI agent to analyze and normalize standards
    • Confirm normalized structure with SME
  2. Week 1, Day 3-5:
    • Request curriculum generation (lessons + assessments)
    • AI agent produces DRAFT documents
    • Distribute to 2-3 SMEs for review
  3. Week 2, Day 1-2:
    • SMEs complete review checklists (30 min each)
    • Collect feedback from all reviewers
    • Compile consolidated feedback
  4. Week 2, Day 3-5:
    • Provide feedback to AI agent
    • AI integrates changes (2-4 hours)
    • Resubmit for final approval
    • Mark as READY when approved

How to Manage SME Reviewers

  • Target: 2-3 SMEs per unit (different perspectives)
  • Time commitment: 30 min per lesson + 20 min for assessment
  • Provide: Lesson page, review checklist, clear deadline
  • Collect: Feedback within 2-3 days
  • Follow up: Thank you + what happens next

For Leadership: Scaling the Project

How to Approve Full Rollout

  1. Review home page for overview
  2. Read LEADERSHIP-SUMMARY.md on GitHub
  3. Review one complete lesson (15 min)
  4. Review assessment to see quality (10 min)
  5. Assess: Cost/benefit, timeline, resource needs
  6. Decide:
    • ☐ Approve pilot (this unit only)
    • ☐ Approve scaling (multiple states/subjects)
    • ☐ Request modifications (specify)
    • ☐ Defer decision (timeline for decision)

Key Metrics to Share

  • 75-85% reduction in teacher creation time
  • 1 week to complete cycle (vs. 2-3 weeks traditional)
  • Quality controlled at every step (SME review gates)
  • Reusable across all states/subjects
  • Scalable from 1 unit to 100+ units

Troubleshooting FAQs

Q: The lesson doesn't match my curriculum pacing guide

A: The timing is flexible. Extend or compress based on your class. Note the change in your review so AI can adjust.

Q: The real-world examples don't match my students' context

A: Suggest better examples in your review. AI will update for regional/cultural relevance.

Q: Can we use these lessons immediately?

A: All lessons are marked DRAFT until SME review is complete. Once you approve, use immediately.

Q: How do we handle copyright or licensing?

A: All content is generated under Mozilla Public License 2.0. See repository for details.

Q: Can we customize lessons before using?

A: Yes! SME feedback and edits are expected. Document changes for future reference.
