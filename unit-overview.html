Standards at a Glance

6.RP.1: Understanding Ratios

Understand the concept of a ratio and use ratio language to describe a ratio relationship between two quantities.

  • ✓ Can identify ratios in real-world contexts
  • ✓ Can write ratios in three forms (3:1, 3 to 1, 3/1)
  • ✓ Understands that order matters

6.RP.2: Unit Rates

Understand the concept of a unit rate a/b associated with a ratio a:b with b ≠ 0, and use rate language in the context of a ratio relationship.

  • ✓ Can calculate unit rates
  • ✓ Can use "per" language correctly
  • ✓ Can compare unit rates to make decisions

6.RP.3: Problem Solving

Use ratio and rate reasoning to solve real-world and mathematical problems, e.g., by reasoning about tables of equivalent ratios, tape diagrams, double number lines, or equations.

  • ✓ Can solve ratio problems using tables
  • ✓ Can solve ratio problems using diagrams
  • ✓ Can select appropriate strategies

Materials Needed

  • Colored tiles or counters (2 colors)
  • Chart paper and markers
  • Ratio and picture cards
  • Manipulatives for ratio representation
  • Real-world examples (menus, prices, recipes)
  • Worksheets and templates (provided)
  • Optional: Digital tools/apps

Cross-Curricular Connections

  • Science: Density, mixing solutions, scale models
  • Social Studies: Map scales, population density, economic comparisons
  • Practical Arts: Cooking, recipe scaling, mixing paints

Differentiation Strategies

For Struggling Learners

  • Use manipulatives and concrete models longer
  • Provide templates and graphic organizers
  • Reduce complexity of problems initially
  • Allow alternative representations

For On-Level Learners

  • Standard activities as designed
  • Progress through strategies in sequence

For Advanced Learners

  • Introduce additional strategies earlier
  • Create word problems for peers
  • Explore complex rate scenarios

Assessment Overview

Formative

  • Exit tickets after each lesson
  • Observation during activities
  • Think-pair-share responses
  • Quick write prompts

Summative

  • Unit assessment (100 points)
  • Multiple choice, short answer, performance tasks
  • Proficiency rubric (3 levels)
