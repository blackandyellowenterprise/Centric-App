Why Centric Changes Everything

Traditional Approach

  • ❌ SMEs create content from scratch
  • ❌ 20-27 hours per curriculum unit
  • ❌ Manual, unstructured review
  • ❌ Inconsistent quality across grades/subjects
  • ❌ 2-3 weeks per unit

Centric Approach

  • ✅ AI generates draft curriculum
  • ✅ SMEs review (1.5-4 hours)
  • ✅ Structured SME review checklist
  • ✅ Consistent, standards-aligned quality
  • ✅ 1 week per unit (or parallel)

Result: 75-85% reduction in teacher creation time
